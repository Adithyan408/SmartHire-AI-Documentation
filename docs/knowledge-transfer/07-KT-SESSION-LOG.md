## 6. Documentation Guidelines

- Update the session record immediately after each KT.
- Record important questions and their answers.
- Include diagrams or relevant references where necessary.
- Assign follow-up actions to specific team members.
- Update the session status.
- Do not mark a session complete until its outcome is documented.
