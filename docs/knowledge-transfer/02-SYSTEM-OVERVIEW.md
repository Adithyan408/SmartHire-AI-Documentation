## 1. Introduction

SmartHire AI is a recruitment and technical interview platform designed to support candidates, recruiters and platform administrators.

The platform combines job and application management with AI-assisted resume screening, interview coordination, live AI-led interviews, coding assessments and structured interview reports.

AI-generated results provide decision support. Recruiters retain responsibility for candidate selection and final hiring decisions.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Simplify recruitment workflows.
- Support candidate and job application management.
- Assist recruiters with resume screening.
- Coordinate candidate interviews.
- Conduct live AI-assisted technical and behavioral interviews.
- Support coding assessments.
- Generate structured interview assessment reports.
- Provide administration and moderation capabilities.

## 3. Application Portals

### 3.1 Candidate Portal

The Candidate Portal provides functionalities for job seekers.

Responsibilities:

- User registration and authentication.
- Profile management.
- Job discovery and browsing.
- Saving jobs.
- Submitting job applications.
- Tracking application and interview status.
- Participating in practice interviews.
- Attending actual recruitment interviews.

### 3.2 Recruiter Portal

The Recruiter Portal supports recruitment and candidate evaluation.

Responsibilities:

- Managing company workspaces.
- Creating, editing and managing job listings.
- Reviewing candidates and resumes.
- Accessing AI resume screening reports.
- Selecting candidates for interviews.
- Managing interview schedules.
- Reviewing assessment reports.
- Making candidate progression and hiring decisions.

### 3.3 Admin Portal

The Admin Portal provides platform-level administration.

Responsibilities:

- Managing users and companies.
- Moderating job listings.
- Handling reports and complaints.
- Managing fraud cases.
- Reviewing platform analytics.
- Managing permissions and audit records.
- Managing platform settings.

## 4. Project Scope

### 4.1 In Scope

- Candidate, recruiter and admin portals.
- Company workspaces and team permissions.
- Job listings and applications.
- AI-assisted resume screening.
- Interview coordination.
- Live AI technical and behavioral interviews.
- Coding workspace integration.
- Structured assessment reports.
- Proctoring and policy-event handling.
- Privacy, security and reliable background processing.

### 4.2 Out of Scope

- Employment offer generation.
- Offer letters and offer approvals.
- Candidate offer acceptance or decline workflows.
- Employer's final in-person or walk-in interview.
- A commitment to a specific cloud provider, database, message broker, AI vendor or deployment platform.

## 5. Main System Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| User Management | Registration, authentication and profile management |
| Job Management | Creating, publishing and managing job listings |
| Application Management | Submitting and tracking applications |
| Resume Screening | AI-assisted comparison of resumes against job requirements |
| Interview Coordination | Invitations, confirmations, reminders and rescheduling |
| Live Interviews | AI-led technical and behavioral interviews |
| Coding Assessment | Executing candidate code in an isolated environment |
| Proctoring | Recording and handling configured interview policy events |
| Assessment Reports | Generating structured interview evaluation reports |
| Administration | Managing platform users, companies and moderation |

## 6. Primary Actors

| Actor | Main Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Candidate | Searches and applies for jobs and attends interviews |
| Recruiter | Manages recruitment and evaluates candidates |
| Administrator | Manages platform operations and moderation |

## 7. Important Product Boundary

AI-generated screening and assessment reports are decision-support tools.

The recruiter remains responsible for candidate selection and final hiring decisions.

Employment offer management and the final offline company interview are outside the current platform scope.
